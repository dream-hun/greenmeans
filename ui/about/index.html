Our Story: From Passion
to Impact

Founded in 2018 by environmental engineers and business strategists, GreenVolt was born from a simple observation:
Businesses wanted to embrace sustainability but lacked the expertise and roadmap to do so effectively. What started as a small consultancy helping local businesses install solar Panels has grown into a comprehensive sustainability partner serving 500+ organisations across North America. Our approach combines environmental science, engineering expertise, and business acumen to deliver solutions that benefit both planet and profit. Today, our team of 40+ certified professionals helps companies of all sizes navigate the complex journey to sustainability. From Fortune 500 corporations to family-owned businesses, we've proven that environmental responsibility and financial success go hand in hand.

Mission & Vision

Our mission is to help businesses grow sustainably. Our vision is a future where companies thrive while making a positive impact on the planet.

Our Mission

Empower businesses to achieve environmental sustainability through expert consulting, innovative solutions, and measurable impact. We make the transition to net-zero operations accessible, profitable, and permanent.

Our Vision

A world where every business operates in harmony with the environment, where sustainability is not a choice but a standard, and where economic growth and environmental preservation advance together.

The Values That Guide Us

We are guided by integrity, sustainability, and innovation, creating positive impact for businesses and the planet.

Value 1

Sustainability First

Every decision we make prioritizes long-term environmental impact over short-term gains. We practice what we preach—our office is 100% solar-powered and carbon-neutral certified.

Value 2

Science-Based Solutions

We rely on data, research, and proven methodologies. All our recommendations are backed by environmental science and industry best practices.

Value 3

Transparency & Integrity

No greenwashing, no exaggeration. We provide honest assessments, realistic timelines, and transparent reporting on all projects.

Value 4

Continuous Innovation

The sustainability landscape evolves rapidly. We stay ahead through constant learning, technology adoption, and innovation in our methodologies.
